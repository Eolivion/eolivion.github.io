Velocity Chart & Sprint Report for Jira Dashboards

Getting started

Velocity Chart & Sprint Report is a widget for the new Jira dashboards. It shows committed and completed for each sprint of a Scrum board, with the average, and opens any sprint's report right on the dashboard. It runs inside Jira and makes no calls to outside servers.

Add the widget

  1. Open a dashboard and choose Edit, then Add widget.
  2. Search for Velocity and add Velocity Chart & Sprint Report.
  3. Pick a Scrum board in its settings, check the preview beside them and choose Add.

The first time, Jira asks you to allow the app to read Jira on your behalf. It only reads: boards, sprints and work items.

The widget is available on the new Jira dashboards. It isn't offered as a gadget on classic dashboards.

Settings

  • Scrum board: the board whose sprints you want to see. Velocity needs sprints, so only Scrum boards are listed.
  • Sprints: the last 6, 12, 18 or 24 closed sprints, or every closed sprint on the board. Jira's own velocity chart stops at 12.
  • Show the active sprint too: adds the sprint in progress at the right. It's left out of the average.

Read the chart

Each sprint has two bars. Committed is what was in the sprint when it started, with the estimates it had then. Completed is what was done when it closed. The dashed line is the average completed across the closed sprints shown.

The numbers use the board's estimate (story points, time or issue count) and come from Jira's own sprint report, counted the same way as Jira's velocity chart.

Open a sprint's report

Click a sprint to see its report below the chart: completed, not completed, removed from the sprint and completed in another sprint, each with its estimate. A + marks work items added after the sprint started, which is why they aren't in the committed total. Click a key to open the work item in Jira.

Who sees what

The widget reads the board and its sprints as the person looking at the dashboard. Everyone sees only the boards and work items they can already open in Jira.
