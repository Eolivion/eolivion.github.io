Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy covers the apps Eolivion builds for Atlassian Cloud: Issues by Team for Jira Dashboards and Planning Poker for Jira. The sections below about "the app" describe Issues by Team; Planning Poker has its own section, because it stores a little more.

The short version

The app reads your Jira data to draw a chart, inside Atlassian's own infrastructure, with your own permissions. Eolivion doesn't receive, store or sell any of it. There are no Eolivion servers involved.

What the app reads

When someone views the gadget, the app asks Jira, on behalf of that person:

  • the list of Teams on the site (id and name), for the chart's slices
  • issue counts for the filter or project picked in the gadget's settings
  • the names of saved filters and projects, only while someone is choosing one in the settings

These requests use the viewer's own Jira permissions. The app can't see anything that person can't already see.

Nothing else is stored. The app doesn't keep copies of issues, team names, counts or user details.

Where the app runs

The app is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's platform. It makes no requests to any server outside Atlassian. Your data stays where your Jira data already is, under Atlassian's hosting, data residency and security commitments.

Planning Poker for Jira

Planning Poker runs entirely on Atlassian's platform too, makes no requests outside Atlassian, and Eolivion can't see its data. Unlike Issues by Team, it keeps some data so a session works for everyone in it.

All of this lives in Atlassian Forge storage, on Atlassian's infrastructure. The app shows names and avatars by asking Jira at the moment they're needed; it doesn't store them.

When someone saves an estimate, the app writes it to the Jira field picked for the session. If that field isn't on the issue's edit screen, the app writes it as itself, but only after checking that the person saving is allowed to edit that issue.

Once a week the app asks Atlassian which of the account IDs it holds belong to closed accounts, and erases their votes, reasons and presence.

What Eolivion receives

When the app is installed, Atlassian shares licensing and contact details about the customer who installs the app (for example, the site name and a billing or technical contact). Eolivion uses these only to manage the license and to answer support requests.

If you contact support, Eolivion keeps your message and contact details for as long as it takes to resolve the request, and for up to 12 months afterwards for reference. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

Sharing

Eolivion doesn't sell or share personal data. The only third party involved is Atlassian, as the host of the app and the Marketplace.

Your rights

You can ask what Eolivion holds about you, ask for a correction or ask for deletion, under the GDPR, the Brazilian LGPD or any similar law that applies to you. Open a request on the support portal. Eolivion will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Material changes will be announced on the app's Marketplace listing.
