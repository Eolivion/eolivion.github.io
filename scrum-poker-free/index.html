Scrum Poker Free by Eolivion

Getting started

Scrum Poker Free by Eolivion lets your team estimate Jira issues together and saves the result to Story points. It's free for teams of up to 10 people, runs inside Jira and makes no calls to outside servers.

Open it

Open a project and pick Scrum Poker Free by Eolivion in the project's navigation (it may be under More). That's the only place the free edition appears.

Start a session

Choose New session, give it a name and pick where the issues come from:

  • Sprint: the open issues of an active or future sprint of a board.
  • Backlog: the project's open issues that aren't in a sprint, in backlog order.
  • JQL: any query, from any projects you can see.
  • Issue keys: paste keys or issue links.

Up to 100 issues per session. While you play you can add, remove and reorder them.

Vote and reveal

Anyone in the project who opens the session can vote. Cards stay hidden until the person running the session reveals them, or until they reveal on their own once everyone present has voted (when that option is on). Then you see the spread, the average, the median and a suggested estimate. Anyone can choose Run this session to take over from the facilitator.

Save to Jira

Pick the final estimate and choose Save and next. It goes to Story points, the field your board uses, detected per issue.

If the field isn't on the issue's edit screen, the app still saves it, after checking that you're allowed to edit that issue. Jira keeps the value and the board, backlog and reports use it. To show it on the issue too, a Jira admin adds the field to that issue type's layout; the app tells you where.

Decks

Fibonacci, modified Fibonacci, powers of 2 or T-shirt sizes. You can change the deck in the middle of a session; votes that weren't saved start over.

Help while estimating

Details shows the description, acceptance criteria, attachments and latest comments, right below the cards.

Teams of up to 10

The free edition is for teams of up to 10 people, counted as the different people who used it on your site in the last 30 days. If more people use it, the app lets you know and gives you 7 days; after that, new sessions pause. Sessions that are already open keep working, including saving to Jira.

Need more?

Scrum Poker by Eolivion has no team limit and adds async voting, relative sizing, prioritization, custom decks, two fields per session, Team sessions, a quick estimate on the issue and session summaries. It's also free for sites with up to 10 users.

Permissions and data

The app reads Jira with the permissions of the person using it, so nobody sees issues they can't already see. Sessions and votes are kept in Atlassian's own storage for the app; see the privacy policy for exactly what's kept and for how long.
