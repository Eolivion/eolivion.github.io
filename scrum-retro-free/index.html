Scrum Retro Free by Eolivion

Getting started

Scrum Retro Free by Eolivion runs your sprint retrospective inside Jira, and the action items become Jira issues. It's free for teams of up to 10 people, runs inside Jira and makes no calls to outside servers.

Open it

Open the project and pick Scrum Retro Free by Eolivion in the project's navigation (it may be under More). That's the only place the free edition appears. Anyone who opens it in that project can join a retro.

Start a retro

  • Sprint: pick the board and the sprint the retro is about, or no sprint.
  • Format: Went well / To improve / Action ideas, Start Stop Continue, Mad Sad Glad, 4Ls, Drop Add Keep Improve, Sailboat, Starfish, KALM or Hopes and Fears.
  • Anonymous cards: nobody sees who wrote what.
  • Votes per person, and whether someone can put more than one vote on the same card.

The steps

  1. Write. Everyone adds cards at the same time. You only see your own cards, so nobody follows the first idea on the board.
  2. Group. All cards show up. Drag a card onto a similar one, or use Group with…, to make one topic out of them.
  3. Vote. Everyone spends their votes on the topics they want to talk about. Totals stay hidden until the discussion.
  4. Discuss. Topics come in vote order. The facilitator picks the one on screen; add action items as you go.
  5. Wrap up. Turn each action item into a Jira issue with one click (it gets the label retro-action).

The person who starts the retro is the facilitator and moves it from step to step. Anyone can choose Run this retro to take over. There's a timer for each step.

Teams of up to 10

The free edition is for teams of up to 10 people, counted as the different people who used it on your site in the last 30 days. If more people use it, the app lets you know and gives you 7 days; after that, new retros pause. Retros that are already open keep working, including turning action items into Jira issues.

Need more?

Scrum Retro by Eolivion has no team limit and adds your own columns, last retro's open action items, the sprint's numbers, an anonymous mood check and a summary to paste into Confluence or Slack. It's also free for sites with up to 10 users.

Permissions and data

The app reads Jira with the permissions of the person using it, and creates issues as that person. Retros, cards and votes are kept in Atlassian's own storage for the app; see the privacy policy for exactly what's kept and for how long.
