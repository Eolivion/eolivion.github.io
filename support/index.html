Support Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Applies to all Eolivion apps on the Atlassian Marketplace, free and paid, including trials.

How to reach support

Open a request on the support portal, or write to support@eolivion.com. The first time you use the portal, create a free account with your email. It takes a few seconds and needs no Atlassian license.

Please include your site address, which app and screen you were on, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Response times

PriorityExampleFirst response
Criticalthe app doesn't load for anyone on the sitewithin 8 business hours
Higha wrong result, or a feature doesn't workwithin 1 business day
Normalquestions, how-to, small issueswithin 1 business day
Feature requestsomething the app doesn't do yetwithin 2 business days

Business hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, Brasília time (UTC−3), except Brazilian national holidays.

"First response" means a person read your request and replied. Fixes take as long as they take, and you'll get an update at least every 2 business days until the request is closed.

Languages

English and Portuguese.

What's covered

  • problems with the app's behavior
  • questions about setting it up and reading the results
  • feature requests. I read all of them, and they decide what gets built next.

Problems in Jira itself are Atlassian's to fix. If one shows up, I'll tell you and, where possible, point you to the right Atlassian ticket.

Security issues

Write to security@eolivion.com. Security reports are handled ahead of everything else.
